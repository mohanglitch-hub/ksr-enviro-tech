Our Journey

Founded in 2021 in Bengaluru, KSR Enviro Tech and Solution was born out of a vision to bridge the gap between industrial water needs and environmental sustainability.

What started as a small, dedicated team of environmental engineers has rapidly grown into a trusted industry partner. Today, we have successfully executed over 100 projects across India, ranging from large-scale industrial Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) to community Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) and advanced Reverse Osmosis (RO) systems.

Our growth is driven by a relentless commitment to quality, timely execution, and a deep understanding of the unique challenges faced by Indian industries and municipalities.
