Not Sure Which You Need?

STP vs ETP vs RO vs WTP

A quick comparison of the four core treatment types — the full picture is always confirmed with a free site survey.

Type What It Treats Typical Use Case Output
STP Domestic sewage from toilets, kitchens & laundry Residential societies, hospitals, hotels, commercial buildings Treated water safe for flushing, gardening or discharge
ETP Industrial process wastewater & effluent Manufacturing, textile & dyeing, food & beverage plants Treated effluent meeting discharge/reuse compliance norms
RO Dissolved salts & impurities in water Drinking water purification, sensitive process water High-purity water for drinking or industrial processes
WTP Raw/source water — borewell, municipal or surface water Any facility needing potable or process-grade water supply Clean input water for domestic or industrial use

Still not sure? Try the capacity estimator or talk to our engineers.
